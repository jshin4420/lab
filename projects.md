# Research Projects

My research program connects **media data, audience behavior, market competition, and policy**. Recent projects focus on how changes in media consumption and platform competition reshape broadcasting and advertising markets.

## Current & Recent Research

### 01 — Media Market Competition
**Broadcasting Market Competition Assessment** · 2022–2025  
**Role:** Co-Principal Investigator · **Funder:** Korea Communications Commission

Long-running research assessing competitive conditions in broadcasting markets, including market definition, concentration, platform competition, and emerging competitive pressure from OTT and digital platforms.

### 02 — Media Diversity Policy
**Improving Institutional Frameworks for Media Diversity** · 2024  
**Role:** Principal Investigator · **Funder:** Korea Communications Commission

Research on institutional approaches to promoting media diversity in an environment of changing audience behavior, platform competition, and media-market structure.

### 03 — Broadcasting Advertising Regulation
**Analysis of the Advertising Market and Revision of Regulations for the Transition of the Broadcasting Advertising Regulatory System** · 2024  
**Role:** Principal Investigator · **Funder:** Korea Communications Commission

Analysis of structural changes in the broadcasting advertising market and implications for the regulatory framework.

### 04 — New Forms of Advertising
**Rationalization of Program Advertising Regulation and the Economic Effects of New Types of Advertising** · 2023  
**Role:** Principal Investigator · **Funder:** Korea Communications Commission

Examining emerging advertising formats and their economic effects, with a focus on the changing relationship between content, advertising, and platform environments.

### 05 — Media-use Measurement
**Comparing Mode Effects in Media-use Surveys** · 2023  
**Role:** Principal Investigator · **Funder:** Korea Information Society Development Institute

Investigating how survey modes affect self-reported media-use measures. This project forms part of a broader methodological research program on the validity and comparability of media-use data.

### 06 — Audience Impact & Advertising Policy
**Audience Impact Assessment of Allowing Mid-roll Advertising on Terrestrial Broadcasting** · 2022  
**Role:** Principal Investigator · **Funder:** Korea Communications Commission

Assessing how changes in advertising policy affect viewing behavior and audience experience.

### 07 — Regulatory Cost & Policy Analysis
**Analysis of Regulatory Costs and Measures to Reduce Costs in Broadcasting and Telecommunications** · 2022  
**Role:** Principal Investigator · **Funder:** Korea Communications Commission

Quantitative analysis of regulatory burdens and policy alternatives in the broadcasting and telecommunications sectors.

## Data & Measurement Infrastructure

### 08 — Korean Media Panel Survey & Broadcasting Statistics DB
**Broadcasting Statistics DB Construction and Operation — Korean Media Panel Survey** · 2019–2020  
**Role:** Co-Principal Investigator · **Funder:** National Research Council for Economics, Humanities and Social Sciences

Large-scale longitudinal media-use data and statistical infrastructure supporting research on media consumption, audience behavior, and changes in the media environment.

### 09 — Audience Evaluation
**KI Viewer Evaluation Index Survey** · 2019–2020  
**Role:** Principal Investigator · **Funder:** Korea Communications Commission

Research and measurement of television audience evaluations and viewing experiences.

### 10 — Broadcasting Industry Data
**Broadcasting Industry Survey & Integrated Broadcasting Statistics Information System** · 2019–2020  
**Role:** Principal Investigator · **Funder:** Ministry of Science and ICT / Korea Communications Commission

Development and operation of statistical resources describing the broadcasting industry and its evolving market structure.

## Methodological & Forecasting Research

### 11 — Future Forecasting Models for ICT & Social Policy
**Development of Future Forecasting Models for National Social Development and ICT/Health & Welfare Policy Support** · 2020–2021  
**Role:** Co-Principal Investigator · **Funder:** National Research Council for Economics, Humanities and Social Sciences

Development of quantitative forecasting approaches for anticipating social and policy change.

### 12 — International Audience Measurement
**Arirang TV Overseas Audience Evaluation Index Survey** · 2020  
**Role:** Co-Principal Investigator · **Funder:** Korea Foundation for International Cultural Exchange

Measurement and analysis of overseas audience perceptions and evaluations of international broadcasting.

## Research Trajectory

These projects represent a longer research trajectory:

**Measurement & Data Infrastructure**  →  **Audience Behavior**  →  **Market Competition**  →  **Platform Change**  →  **Policy & Regulation**

Current work extends this trajectory toward **cross-platform substitution, audience fragmentation, TV–OTT relationships, digital advertising, and statistical methodology for platform-based media research**.
