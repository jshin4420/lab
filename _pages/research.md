## Audiences · Platforms · Markets · Methods

Our research examines how media use changes as audiences move across an increasingly fragmented platform ecosystem.

### 01 — Audience Fragmentation

How are audiences distributed across television, OTT, YouTube, and emerging platforms?

### 02 — Cross-platform Substitution

When audiences move between platforms, are they substituting one medium for another—or supplementing existing media use?

### 03 — Media Market Competition

How do changing audience behaviors reshape competition among broadcasters, platforms, and advertising markets?

### 04 — Statistical Methodology

Developing quantitative frameworks for measuring substitution, overlap, and platform dynamics in media-use data.
