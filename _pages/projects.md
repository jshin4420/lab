## Research Projects

Our projects connect applied media-policy research with quantitative methods for understanding audiences, platforms, and markets.

### 01 — Audience & Media Use

**Korean Media Panel Survey · Media-use measurement · Audience behavior**

Longitudinal research on how people use television, OTT, and other media, with a focus on measurement, behavioral change, and audience fragmentation.

**Selected projects**

- *Comparing Mode Effects in Media-use Surveys* — PI, KISDI, 2023
- *Korean Media Panel Survey / Broadcasting Statistics DB* — Co-PI, 2019–2020
- *KI Viewer Evaluation Index Survey* — PI, 2019–2020

### 02 — Media Markets & Advertising

**Competition · Advertising · Broadcasting · Digital platforms**

Research on competitive conditions in broadcasting and advertising markets, including the growing influence of OTT and digital platforms.

**Selected projects**

- *Analysis of the Advertising Market and Regulatory Reform* — PI, 2024
- *Broadcasting Market Competition Assessment* — Co-PI, 2022–2025
- *Rationalization of Program Advertising Regulation and Economic Effects of New Types of Advertising* — PI, 2023
- *Audience Impact Assessment of Mid-roll Advertising on Terrestrial Broadcasting* — PI, 2022
- *Broadcasting Industry Survey* — PI, 2019–2020

### 03 — Statistical Methodology

**Survey integration · Statistical matching · Measurement · Mixed-mode surveys**

Developing and applying statistical methods that address the measurement challenges created by heterogeneous surveys and increasingly complex media-use data.

**Selected projects**

- *Comparing Mode Effects in Media-use Surveys* — PI, 2023
- *Broadcasting Statistics DB / Korean Media Panel Survey* — Co-PI
- *Development of Future Forecasting Models for National Social Development and ICT/Health & Welfare Policy Support* — Co-PI, 2020–2021
