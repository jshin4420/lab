<section class="lab-section lab-question">
  <div class="lab-section-label">RESEARCH</div>
  <div>
    <h2>Four questions guide<br>our research.</h2>
    <p>Our work connects audience behavior, platform dynamics, media markets, and statistical methodology.</p>
  </div>
</section>

<div class="lab-research-grid">
  <article><span>01</span><h3>Audience<br>Fragmentation</h3><p>How are audiences distributed across television, OTT, YouTube, and emerging platforms?</p></article>
  <article><span>02</span><h3>Cross-platform<br>Substitution</h3><p>When audiences move between platforms, are they substituting one medium for another—or adding new forms of media use?</p></article>
  <article><span>03</span><h3>Media Market<br>Competition</h3><p>How do changing audience behaviors reshape competition among broadcasters, platforms, and advertising markets?</p></article>
  <article><span>04</span><h3>Statistical<br>Methodology</h3><p>How can statistical frameworks capture substitution, overlap, measurement differences, and platform dynamics?</p></article>
</div>

<section class="lab-statement">
  <p>The question is no longer simply <strong>what people watch</strong>, but how their media choices interact across an increasingly fragmented ecosystem.</p>
</section>

<section class="lab-profile">
  <div class="lab-section-label">PRINCIPAL INVESTIGATOR</div>
  <div>
    <h2>Statistical methodology<br>for complex social data.</h2>
    <p><strong>Jihyung Shin, Ph.D.</strong> is a quantitative researcher with a Ph.D. in Statistics from Florida State University. Since joining KISDI in 2012, her work has combined statistical methodology and computational data analysis with research on media, audiences, markets, and public policy.</p>
    <p>Her methodological research includes statistical matching, survey data integration, multiple imputation, mixed-mode measurement, mixed-effects models, zero-inflated models, correlated random effects, and simulation-based evaluation.</p>
